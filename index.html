Contact

Common questions

How do I play? Hold anywhere on the screen to tiptoe. Let go to freeze. If the boss looks up while you are moving, you are caught.

How do I unlock the next day? Clear the day before it. Overtime mode opens after Day 3.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone? Tap Save code, copy the code, and paste it into Load code on the new phone.

Does the game cost anything? No. It is free, with no ads and no accounts.
