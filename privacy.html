Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Don't Wake the Boss is a single-player game. It is built to collect nothing about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (stars, best times, coins, leave days, promotions and items from the in-game shop) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

Save codes

If you use Save code, the game shows your progress as a text code. It only leaves your device if you copy and share it yourself.

Sharing

If you tap Share score, your phone's share sheet opens with a short message. You choose whether and where to send it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the App Store privacy details before the change goes live.
